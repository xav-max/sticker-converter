Platform Output Specifications

WhatsApp
  • Format: Animated WebP (.webp, recommended) or GIF
  • Size: 512×512 px (min 85% inner scale), ≤ 500 KB (tray icon: 96×96 px, ≤ 50 KB)
  • Framerate & Duration: ≤ 10s duration, min 8 ms/frame (3–30 stickers/pack)
WeChat
  • Format: GIF
  • Size: Max 240×240 px (cover.png: 240×240, icon.png: 50×50), ≤ 500 KB
  • Framerate & Duration: Dynamic adjustment, optimized palette
LINE
  • Format: APNG
  • Size: 320×270 max (main.gif: 240×240, tab.png: 96×74), ≤ 300 KB (main ≤ 1 MB)
  • Framerate & Duration: 5–20 frames, strictly 1, 2, 3, or 4s, loops within 4s
Telegram
  • Format: WEBM (VP9 + Alpha)
  • Size: 512×512 max, ≤ 256 KB
  • Framerate & Duration: Max 30 FPS, max 3s
Signal
  • Format: APNG
  • Size: 512×512 px (min 70% inner scale), ≤ 300 KB
  • Framerate & Duration: Max 30 FPS, max 3s
GIPHY (Instagram / TikTok)
  • Format: Animated GIF (loop: Forever, ≥ 2 frames)
  • Transparency: Required — ≥ 20% transparent pixels on 1st frame
  • Size & Duration: 512×512 px recommended, ≤ 100 MB, ≤ 6s recommended (15s max)
